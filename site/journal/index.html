Journal

1300-06-15, 1300-06-16

The party infiltrated the Blood Order Headquarters and managed to defeat the denizens found within, including the leader Beliar. Before Beliar could be questioned, the Masked Man, disguised as Aidhem, dealt the finishing blow, preventing Natalie from learning what she needed to help Amanda.

After a brief rest in the dungeon, Curt woke up with a strange seal on the back of his neck.

After returning to Dimok, Reymar read a book detailing the 1016 war in Irehonia, written by Antoine. Reymar realized that the events of the war (relative to Irehonia), as known by the general populace, weren’t entirely accurate. Irehonia actually played a significant role in weakening Laszlo’s forces by effectively crippling his demonic army. Additionally, Reymar learned that Bagrag l, was Baltrin’s father.

That same day, Aidhem’s corpse was found in the underground canals with his face missing.

Finally, Shiro joined Infallible.

1300-06-17

For most of the day, Reymar and Curt studied magic. Curt was victorious at his card game. Infallible won their match that day. Afterwards, most characters attended the ball, where Reymar served as the governor’s personal bodyguard. Later in the night, Reymar lured the governor to his room, where Amelia ended up killing him, taking advantage of the chaos taking place downstairs. She wounded Reymar to make it look as if he tried to protect the governor. Afterwards, Matilde was found alive in the City Hall by Curt and Shiro and brought to the party's quarters.

1300-06-18

Everyone present at the previous night’s ball was interrogated regarding the assassination of the governor. Reymar spoke to Aiden, who suggested that he throw the game, seeing as Infallible was expected to win. Reymar then talked with the new captain, Kayla, where he was forced to tell the truth about the previous night’s events.

Curt attempted to learn more about the Masked Man after his interrogation and considered weakening his influence on Defiant members by substituting the "iodine" consumed by them with a harmless substance. Shiro spoke with Lady Jennifer Gray about House Claville.

We opened the adamantium lock that held the chain around the sword. Lord Germain Duncan is the soul trapped in the sword, who lived 300 years ago. Gelemer has a book that he wants destroyed in the name of the Nameless Protector.

Infallible was victorious against Vitriol after they got disqualified when Aiden destroyed Eve the Golem after Natalie had forfeited. Reymar had to convince the team to not fall apart after Curt and Shiro blamed Natalie for forfeiting, despite Reymar revealing it was his own idea all along and taking the blame.

1300-06-19

Reymar was told by Father Peter that he was needed to aid the wounded in a tavern in West Gate, where a fight had broken out. It turned out to be a ruse when Reymar was ambushed by Aiden’s men. At one point, Shiro tried to counterspell a black opal cannon used by one of the assassins. The device's safety system was disrupted, resulting in an uncontrolled blast of mass disintegration, which, if not stopped, would likely destroy the entire neighborhood. Fortunately, Yahlra appeared (as well as Nylha), and teleported the blast somewhere else with her special power. The leader of the assassins was taken alive and promised to lead us to his boss.

Reymar told Baltrin about his past before the match with Legacy, infuriating him. Unfortunately, despite the game being very close, Legacy was ultimately victorious. This hit Baltrin particularly hard, since Bagrag II was his rival.

After the match, Curt, Reymar, Shiro, No-one, and the prisoner went into the sewers to meet the prisoner’s boss, Sawyer. Reymar convinced him that he was dead, after which, Shiro suggested they all go to a tavern to celebrate. Shiro got Sawyer very drunk and convinced him to go to Infallible’s headquarters, since it was nearby. Afterwards, he was placed in chains.

That night, Reymar had another dream where the man wearing a turban appeared. In the dream, he was passing through the halls of the Blood Orders HQ, and behind every door was a faceless corpse of someone killed by the Masked Man. In Beliar’s chamber, he saw a map showing that the Blood Order’s influence was widespread. Afterwards, Reymar ended up in a burning Dimok engulfed in chaos. The Masked Man appeared, singing a song describing the destruction. When Reymar looked at his reflection in a puddle, he saw that he himself was wearing a mask.

1300-06-20

Sawyer was interrogated after he woke up. Unfortunately, he was unwilling to devolve information until Shiro cast a spell on him forcing him to tell the truth. He told the party that while he couldn’t set up a meeting with the king, he knew of someone that could. He then revealed other relevant information about the Syndicate.

Reymar left on his own, disguised as an older wizard, to run some errands. First, he found Baltrin and consoled him. Ultimately, he persuaded him to keep fighting. Then, he went to the university to take the Element of Darkness Exam. After cheating numerous times, Reymar ultimately passed the test, finding out that his actions were actually what was expected of him.

Meanwhile, Shiro and Curt set off to search the House Claville Manor in Tales. They successfully infiltrated the compound using stealth and returned to Dimok with a chest, which turned out to be heavily trapped. Afterwards, Sawyer was freed after Shiro cast a spell making him misremember certain events. When Reymar found out about this, he was rather cross.
